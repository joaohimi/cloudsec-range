# Curriculum Coverage Policy — Zero Curriculum Gaps

## Non-negotiable rule

Every topic in the user's canonical study map receives the same quality standard in the Learning Center and Internship Center. Equal rigor does not mean identical lesson length; it means each topic is complete relative to its technical scope, prerequisites and risk.

## Required learning coverage

For every competency, record:

1. Definition, purpose and prerequisites.
2. Complete progressive explanation and terminology.
3. Internal behavior and relevant standards/protocols.
4. Official documentation and version context.
5. Practical examples.
6. Guided lab.
7. Independent exercise.
8. Troubleshooting scenario.
9. Security relevance and controls, where applicable.
10. Theory and practical assessment.
11. Evidence and review history.

## Required internship coverage

For every competency, provide an appropriate professional application:

1. Company context and asset/service involved.
2. Role and responsibility.
3. Service request, ticket, incident or change request.
4. Initial state and reproducible environment/data.
5. Investigation and evidence collection.
6. Execution or remediation procedure.
7. Validation and acceptance criteria.
8. Technical review and documentation.
9. Competency evidence and feedback.

A single ticket may cover multiple related competencies, but each competency must have traceable evidence. Foundational topics must have internship experiences too. Example: disk, partition, filesystem, mount, fstab, LVM, inode and storage troubleshooting map to realistic systems-administration tickets before they later connect to AWS EBS/EFS/S3.

## Mastery policy

A topic is mastered only when the learner can explain it, apply it, troubleshoot it and demonstrate the result independently. Viewing content or passing a multiple-choice quiz is insufficient.

## Canonical curriculum integrity

The original study map is the source of truth. Do not invent module names, reorder modules, add hours or mark missing modules as complete. The exact 28-module / 9-block Linux map and other canonical maps must be imported and validated before initial curriculum seeding.
